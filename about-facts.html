Background, Credentials & Topics

Bio, career background, and areas of focus.

Speaker Bio

Joerg Proeve is a global insurance technology and strategy executive whose career spans carriers (Chubb, CNA), MGA startups, and management consulting and advisory. He led US operations as COO of BOXX Insurance, a cyber MGA later acquired by Zurich, and founded Parachute Insurance, a parametric MGA for hurricane risk. At CNA he designed the governance framework for a $325M technology portfolio. At Chubb he led strategy initiatives and post-merger integration workstreams following the $28B acquisition. He holds an MBA from London Business School and a Master's in Electrical Engineering from Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Germany. He writes regularly on insurance technology, cyber insurance, AI liability, and the MGA market structure in Insurance Thought Leadership, American Banker, and the Breezy Risk Advisors Insights blog.

Speaking Topics

MGA Market Structure

How the MGA model is scaling, why carriers are acquiring underwriting platforms, and what the consolidation wave means for capacity, distribution, and operations.

AI Liability & Emerging Risk

The gap between AI liability products coming to market and the measurement infrastructure underneath them. What insurers learned from cyber that applies to AI, and what doesn't.

Carrier Technology Strategy

Where technology investments generate underwriting advantage versus where they just generate cost. Lessons from governing a $325M portfolio and integrating two legacy stacks.

Insurance Operating Models

What is becoming abundant in insurance, what remains scarce, and how the economics of the carrier-MGA-reinsurer stack are shifting.
