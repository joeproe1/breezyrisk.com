Joerg Proeve

Who I Am

I'm Joerg Proeve. I have spent my career on both sides of the insurance operating model, inside carriers and building MGAs.

At Chubb, I worked in corporate strategy and then post-merger integration after the $28B ACE-Chubb merger, consolidating the two legacy organizations and introducing a new performance reporting framework across 54 countries. At CNA, I designed the governance framework for its $325M technology portfolio: prioritizing investments, structuring steering committee decisions, and selecting vendor partnerships at scale. Earlier, I advised insurance CEOs on corporate strategy and value creation at Marakon and Kearney.

I have also built insurance from the ground up. As COO of BOXX Insurance, a cyber MGA, I built operations from product architecture through distribution across all 50 states; the company was later acquired by Zurich. Before that I founded Parachute Insurance, a parametric MGA for hurricane risk.

Today I work with carriers, MGAs, and investors on insurance strategy, technology, and operations.

My background bridges business and engineering: MBA from London Business School, UK, and Master's in Electrical Engineering from Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Germany. I started my career in cybersecurity at Siemens.

What I Work On

I founded Breezy Risk Advisors to bring operator experience to the industry's decision-makers: investors evaluating insurance deals, carriers navigating technology strategy, and startups building in emerging risk.

Recent work includes MGA platform diligence and capacity economics analysis for investment firms, and technology strategy reviews for insurers evaluating build-versus-buy decisions.
