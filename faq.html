Frequently Asked Questions

For Investors

What does your investor advisory work cover?

▼

Deal screening, market analysis, and portfolio company operations for PE and VC funds evaluating insurance businesses and insurtech vendors. I help investors understand MGA unit economics, capacity structures, carrier technology stacks, and operating model risks.

The work draws on direct experience building and running insurance operations at Chubb, CNA, and two MGA startups.

What insurance sectors do you cover for investors?

▼

Broadly across P&C: MGA platforms, carrier technology, insurtech and data vendors, cyber, excess casualty, parametric and catastrophe risk, and emerging lines like AI liability. My operating background spans carriers, MGAs, and startups: I governed a $325M technology portfolio at CNA, ran US operations for a cyber MGA acquired by Zurich, and founded a parametric MGA for hurricane risk.

For Carriers & Insurtechs

What carrier technology strategy work have you done?

▼

At CNA, I designed the governance framework for a $325M technology portfolio, covering investment prioritization, steering committee structure, and vendor partnership selection at scale. At Chubb, I worked in corporate strategy and post-merger integration following the $28B ACE-Chubb merger, consolidating legacy organizations and introducing performance reporting across 54 countries.

I also advised insurance CEOs on strategy and value creation at Marakon and Kearney.

How do you advise on emerging risks like AI liability?

▼

I have built insurance products around two emerging risk classes: cyber and parametric catastrophe. That experience informs how I evaluate new risk categories like AI liability, autonomous systems, and algorithmic decision-making.

The advisory work covers product feasibility, coverage architecture, and the measurement infrastructure needed to underwrite risks that do not yet have mature loss data.

For MGAs

What is your experience with MGA operations?

▼

I served as COO of BOXX Insurance, a cyber MGA where I built operations from product architecture through distribution across all 50 states. The company was later acquired by Zurich. Before that I founded Parachute Insurance, a parametric MGA for hurricane risk.

That combination of building from scratch and scaling through acquisition gives me a practical view of MGA operations that goes beyond consulting frameworks.
